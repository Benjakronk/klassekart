# Research foundation

Peer-reviewed basis for Klassekart's statistics + "aims → tips" features. Each
finding is paired with the in-app action it justifies. Citations are for the
teacher-facing tips library and for designing what we measure — not decoration.

## Findings → in-app action

| Finding (source) | What it justifies in-app |
|---|---|
| Affective **teacher–student relationships** have medium–large links to engagement, smaller but real links to achievement; negative relationships especially harmful (Roorda et al., 2011, *Review of Educational Research*) | The teacher–student relationship layer; flag students lacking a positive adult relationship; prompt teachers to reframe "red/yellow" relationships. |
| **Relationship Mapping**: deliberately ensuring every student has ≥1 positive adult connection reduces dropout/bullying, improves SEL; run ~twice/year (Making Caring Common, Harvard GSE) | Import & surface the school's green/yellow/red/white session; headline indicator "every student has ≥1 green adult." |
| **Classroom-management** programs: small but real effects (avg g≈0.22); **social-emotional-focused** strategies help most for SEL, **teacher-focused** for academics (Korpershoek et al., 2016, *RER*; update 2025) | Two-headline framing (climate vs work-done); tips split into relational vs structural levers. |
| **Cooperative learning** works best with structured, often **heterogeneous** groups; long-term heterogeneous "base groups" support academic + social growth (Johnson, Johnson & Stanne, 2000) | Heterogeneous-grouping aim; tag-based balancing; "good group" = mixed, not clustered. |
| **School belonging** is linked to motivation, wellbeing, behaviour, and achievement (Allen et al., 2018; school-belonging meta-analyses) | Belonging as a climate indicator; social-reach / no-isolates metrics; student preference input. |
| **Seating & engagement**: proximity to instructor and decentralized/semicircular layouts raise participation; team-based seating affects engagement (peer-reviewed quasi-experiments, e.g. PMC8814094; ERIC EJ1194750). Back-row disadvantage widely reported (weaker/secondary sources — treat as indicative). | Front/back equity metric; layout-aware tips; "rotate back-row students forward." |
